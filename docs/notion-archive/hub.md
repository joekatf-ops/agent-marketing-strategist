# How the system fits together

1. Establish business economics, product truth, customer evidence and market context.
2. Define each concept coordinate using one Who paired with one primary Problem.
3. Build four executions for the same coordinate: Unaware, Problem Aware, Solution Aware and Product Aware.
4. Launch one concept per ad set in an ABO creative-testing campaign so each concept receives a protected learning budget.
5. Observe for five full days, then diagnose the business, funnel and creative signals.
6. Scale proven winners, build evidence-led iterations and retain the learning for the next cycle.

# Why this structure exists

A new brand can optimise for immediate efficiency or for learning. At the beginning, this system prioritises learning. ABO protects budget at the concept level, allowing meaningful hypotheses about the market, message, format and destination to be tested instead of guessed.

The aim is not only to find an ad that works. The aim is to understand what the market responds to and why. Once clear, repeatable messaging and proven winners emerge, the objective shifts toward efficient distribution through a secondary CBO scaling campaign.

> ABO creates the learning. CBO converts that learning into scale.

# Locked operating rules

- Concept coordinate = one Who x one primary Problem.
- Awareness, messaging route, hook, format, proof, offer, visual execution and destination are execution-level choices.
- Every initial concept test contains four ads: Unaware, Problem Aware, Solution Aware and Product Aware.
- Most Aware is handled by the page, product and offer environment rather than a standard ad.
- Testing intentionally begins with large differences across awareness, format and destination. Later testing narrows variables as evidence improves.
- The testing budget is never below $50 per ad set per day. Approximately $100 is preferred, with higher budgets set from product economics and account context.
- The initial observation window is five full days.
- Core documentation remains brand-neutral. [brand name redacted] appears only in clearly labelled examples.
