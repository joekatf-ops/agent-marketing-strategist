# The four-ad structure

Every initial concept test contains four ads. All four retain the same Who x Problem coordinate while changing the awareness entry point, format and destination.

> Recognition -> Diagnosis -> Differentiation -> Decision

# 1. Unaware: Recognition

Customer state: they experience the symptom, situation or behaviour but have not connected it to an underlying problem.

Messaging job:

- Reflect a recognisable experience
- Create curiosity
- Help the customer notice or name the issue
- Introduce the product later rather than leading with it

Default destination: landing page.

# 2. Problem Aware: Diagnosis

Customer state: they recognise the problem but do not yet understand what could solve it.

Messaging job:

- Name the problem precisely
- Explain why it persists
- Introduce the principle behind the solution
- Move from frustration to a clearer diagnosis

Default destination: landing page.

# 3. Solution Aware: Differentiation

Customer state: they know solutions exist and are comparing approaches.

Messaging job:

- Explain the category or mechanism
- Show why this approach is different
- Compare alternatives fairly
- Support the product with proof and demonstration

Default destination: product detail page.

# 4. Product Aware: Decision

Customer state: they know the product but still have doubts, objections or alternatives.

Messaging job:

- Build trust
- Resolve the main objection
- Demonstrate fit and value
- Use proof, guarantee and offer information to support the decision

Default destination: product detail page.

# Most Aware

Most Aware remains part of awareness theory, but it is not a standard ad in this operating system. It is handled through the landing page, product page, offer and conversion environment.

# Execution diversity

The initial four ads intentionally make large creative swings. Awareness, format and destination may change together. This is a performance-led market test, not a laboratory experiment.

The four executions should be visibly and structurally distinct. Useful formats include:

- UGC or creator-led video
- Educational VSL or voiceover
- Founder, expert or demonstration
- Static image
- Carousel or structured comparison

Format is selected after the Who, Problem and awareness job are clear.

# Destination rules

Standard routing:

- Unaware -> LP
- Problem Aware -> LP
- Solution Aware -> PDP
- Product Aware -> PDP

Other deliberate destinations, including HP, can be used when the ad and page remain congruent. Destination is always recorded in the ad name.

# Standalone-ad rule

Meta does not guarantee sequencing. Any awareness execution may be the first or only ad a person sees. Every ad must therefore make a complete argument without relying on another ad for context.

# How testing narrows

Initial testing explores large differences to discover useful territory. Once the market response becomes clearer, ITR batches narrow the variables to improve the message and diagnose why it works.
