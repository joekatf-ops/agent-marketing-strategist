# Testing objective

The first objective is learning. Efficiency remains important, but early tests exist to establish which markets, messages and executions deserve further investment.

A test should prove or disprove a useful hypothesis rather than simply generate activity.

# Initial review window

- Run for five full days.
- Respect the planned ad-set budget.
- Do not make routine creative changes during the observation window.
- Intervene only for an operational failure, broken destination, policy issue or unacceptable commercial risk.

# What the test can establish

A broad initial test can show:

- Whether the Who x Problem coordinate has commercial potential
- Which complete execution attracts the strongest delivery and response
- Whether the likely weakness sits in the ad, page, offer or economics
- What should be stopped, repeated or developed

It cannot prove that awareness, format, hook or destination alone caused the result because those elements deliberately change together.

# Three measurement layers

## 1. Business

Review:

- Spend
- Purchases
- CAC
- Revenue
- Contribution after advertising
- Volume relative to target economics

Question: can this coordinate acquire customers at useful economics?

## 2. Funnel

Review:

- Outbound CTR
- Landing-page views
- Add-to-cart rate
- Checkout rate
- Conversion rate
- Ad-to-page message match

Question: is the weakness in attention, the click, the page, the explanation or the offer?

## 3. Creative

Review:

- Spend distribution
- Hook retention and watch behaviour
- Comments and qualitative response
- Awareness framing
- Messaging route
- Format and creator
- Proof and visual execution

Question: what did the complete execution teach us, and what should be made next?

# Decision framework

## Financial winner

Meaning: at or below target CAC with useful volume.

Action: continue, graduate to scaling and build evidence-led ITR batches.

## Directional promise

Meaning: above target CAC, but meaningful attention, clicks, conversion or customer response provides a signal.

Action: diagnose the weakness and build an ITR.

## Interest with weak conversion

Meaning: the ad earns attention or traffic, but the page or offer fails.

Action: review message match, education, proof, destination and offer.

## Weak throughout

Meaning: no meaningful commercial or creative signal.

Action: stop, archive the learning and make a larger NNT.

## Initial winner with scale failure

Meaning: performance deteriorates materially at higher spend.

Action: record the scale result separately, then broaden, iterate or retire.

## Winner at scale

Meaning: acceptable economics continue as spend increases.

Action: retain in the scaling campaign and evergreen winner library.

# Required learning record

For every test, record:

1. What was tested.
2. What happened.
3. The strongest and weakest executions.
4. The likely explanation.
5. Confidence in that explanation.
6. The next action: keep, ITR, stop or scale.

A losing test is still valuable when it produces a clear retained learning.
