# Marketing strategist: current state after the Scientific Advertising update

Date: 13 September 2026. Method version: 1.5.0. This describes implemented instructions and bounded
validation, not a claim that the method increases conversion or works identically on every model.

## What changed

The approved audit recommendations have working equivalents in the skill. The main change is a
consistent selling-usefulness check: relevant buyer, wanted experience, useful selling information,
evidence and next action. The moment-to-meaning framework remains central. Emotion, curiosity and
stakes are tools to choose, not a compulsory quota. A direct product fact can be a good opening.

Specificity is separate from exclusivity. Open loops must lead to worthwhile answers. Copy length
must preserve necessary explanation, proof and material terms. Image, headline, body and destination
share one coherent argument, and each awareness execution must be understandable independently.

The test method now includes an explicit control, exact change, constants, allocation, commercial
outcome, exposure, decision rule and limitations. Healthy hook rate does not establish that creative
works; high attributed revenue does not establish profitability or isolate the offer as the fault.

Practical guides now cover samples and paid trials, enquiry follow-up, education and behaviour-change
cost, product availability, channel incrementality and buyer-facing descriptors. These are optional
routes, so product information plus a prompt still produces useful work without a research project.

## Remaining gaps, in priority order

1. **Real commercial validation.** We need controlled campaign observations with adequate exposure,
   trustworthy conversion tracking and contribution/refund data to learn which appeals sell.
   Craft scores and owner approval do not supply this evidence. The skill plans and diagnoses;
   it does not automatically launch or run Meta experiments.
2. **Brand-specific proof.** Keep product facts, offer terms, customer accounts and approved claims
   traceable and current. For Cadian, wanting fewer awakenings or more daytime energy does not
   establish that grounding sheets deliver those outcomes. Better copy cannot fill an evidence gap.
3. **Headline quality and broader output testing.** The new six-scenario use check is small and qualitative. Repeated
   cross-model tests, more product categories, explicit worthwhile-answer tests and matched thin-input
   versus genuine customer-research briefs are still needed. The blind review found both versions
   could drift into product details and lose audience relevance. The final guidance explicitly
   addresses this, but the final two-brief check still returned product-led Cadian headlines.
   Preserving audience relevance under a strict claim ceiling is therefore an observed remaining
   weakness, alongside headline originality and concision.
   No broad quality lift is established.
4. **Live tool and image-route verification.** This update did not rerun Foreplay, Higgsfield,
   ChatGPT or Nano Banana generation. Each host needs capability checks, returned-model verification,
   actual dimension checks and visual review. Earlier reported model mismatches remain relevant;
   no new like-for-like provider result is claimed here.
5. **Depth and automation beyond Meta creative.** Trial operations, enquiry delivery, retail rollout,
   multilingual naming validation and statistical analysis have planning guidance rather than full
   execution integrations. Add those when a concrete business use calls for them.
6. **Context efficiency.** The full writing bundle is roughly 154 KB and the complete library about
   446 KB. Both can be excessive for a small task or constrained runtime. The image edition remains
   below its existing 90,000-byte limit; filesystem runtimes should load relevant references only.
   Further modular packaging is an opportunity, not a prerequisite for using the skill.

## How to use it now

For this installed agent, invoke the marketing strategist with the product, task and available
sources. For another text-capable LLM, use PROMPT.md with the focused bundle needed for the task;
for image work, the self-contained dist/image-ad-bundle.md carries the core checks and image workflow.
A text-only host can produce copy and prompts, not rendered or inspected images. Full reference
examples remain in the standalone guides and larger bundles; the image edition omits extended
examples and duplicate commentary to preserve its size limit.

The most useful next Cadian exercise is one existing control against one new appeal or headline,
with the exact product facts, supporting body, destination and measurement plan recorded before
production. Choose a single-variable or package comparison honestly, based on what actually changes.

## Validation and the limits of the review

- 250 automated tests passed, including new checks for changed-rubric comparisons, incomplete or
  invalid judge scores and focused image-bundle extraction. These are engineering checks, not
  evidence of writing quality. Stubbed perfect scores in their logs are fixtures, not model results.
- Package, skill, copy-lexicon and image-record validation passed. Generated entrypoints and bundles
  were rebuilt; the final image edition is 89,908 bytes against its existing 90,000-byte limit.
- Optional live-eval configuration was exercised with dummy inputs: missing configuration skips
  the paid evaluation explicitly; configured run failures remain errors. No external model API
  evaluation was run locally for this release.
- Two isolated agents answered the same six requests using a 1.4.2 snapshot and an intermediate
  1.5.0 snapshot. A separate reviewer received shuffled left/right answers without version labels.
  The intermediate update was preferred for controlled test planning and product descriptors;
  1.4.2 was preferred narrowly for Cadian headlines and the simple organiser concept; trial/follow-up
  and commercial diagnosis tied. That is two preferences each and two ties, not an overall win.
- The original requests, exact answers, shuffled pairs, decode key and review are retained under
  evals/qualitative/2026-09-13. This is one qualitative sample using inherited agent configuration,
  not a repeated or statistically controlled model comparison. Source snapshots were isolated.
- Final cleanup then aligned the low-awareness guide and hook-format defaults with the new gate,
  and explicitly retained the reader's situation when the evidence limits the promised outcome.
  Those final edits were not part of the blinded comparison.

These results justify a more coherent method and better-specified planning tools. They do not justify
a claim of better headlines across the board, universal LLM compatibility or higher sales.

A final fresh agent used the completed instructions on the Cadian and organiser creative briefs.
The organiser concept was concise and factual. The Cadian set still prioritised setup/material
curiosity over the requested tired-morning situation. The outputs are retained as
final-creative-check.json. This weakness remains visible rather than being counted as a pass or
concealed by the package checks. The next improvement should use targeted examples and genuine
brand evidence to test that balance, not add further generic headline rules.
