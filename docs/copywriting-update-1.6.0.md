Scientific Advertising method with the four reviewed video sources. Their transcripts remain
local research material; only original synthesis, source links and worked edits ship in the skill.

## What now changes the writing process

- Choose one central argument before writing. Supporting facts reinforce it.
- Preserve a chosen concept when generating headline options or rewriting. Different wording can
  be useful without pretending it is a new strategic concept.
- Establish the useful answer before creating curiosity. Qualify the intended reader and deliver
  that answer in the next beat or destination.
- Translate abstract benefits into recognisable moments and practical uses. Ordinary product
  facts remain useful; exclusivity, numbers and dramatic claims are not requirements.
- Make a separate editorial pass after drafting: compare actual candidates, rewrite the strongest
  expressions and remove filler while preserving the thought.
- Review headline, image, support and destination together. Concept exploration and finished
  product promises have different evidence requirements; neither factual drift nor invented
  efficacy is a successful response to the brief.

Product information or a website/PDP remains sufficient for a useful first draft. Research adds
traceable language, situations, objections and evidence when available. It is not a required form
to complete before producing copy.

## Practical limits

The method is updated and usable, but does not guarantee strong copy on every run. Continue judging
actual headlines in context. Cadian outcome claims still need appropriate evidence; an attractive
direction is not an approved sleep claim. Real campaign testing, broader model testing and current
image-provider verification remain separate work. The full writing and knowledge bundles are large;
use the standalone prompt or relevant references for a small task.
