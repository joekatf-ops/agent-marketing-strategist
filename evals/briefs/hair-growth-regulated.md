# Brief: hair density supplement, regulated claim territory

- Product: a daily supplement marketed for hair thickness and density
- Category: health and wellness
- Market: United States
- Price: 39 USD per month on subscription
- Mechanism: saw palmetto, biotin and marine collagen
- Proof available: a 90-day self-reported user survey, n of 112, run by the brand. No clinical trial
- Approved claims: supports normal hair growth. Prohibited: regrows hair, reverses hair loss, any
  before and after imagery
- Awareness target: PRA. The reader has noticed thinning
- Deliverable: hook options for video, and note the policy risk on each
- Production limits: creator on camera, no scalp close-ups approved
