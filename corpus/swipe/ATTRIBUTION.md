# Attribution and removal

This corpus records advertising that ran publicly on Meta, for the purpose of analysing how it was
constructed. Each entry carries the brand, a link to the ad on Foreplay, and a written reading of the
technique it uses.

## What is recorded, and why

Headlines, primary text and transcripts appear verbatim because the analysis is about the exact
words. Paraphrasing a hook to avoid quoting it would make the annotation useless: the reading is of
the specific construction, not a summary of the idea.

Advertising is published material, and this is commentary and teaching about published material. The
brands here are studied because their work is good.

## What this is not

Not an endorsement, a partnership or any suggestion of affiliation. Not a claim that any ad performed
well commercially: this corpus has no access to performance data, and how long an ad ran indicates
only that somebody kept funding it.

**Every claim in a corpus ad belongs to that brand.** The reference library states this in several
places and it is the rule that matters most here. Reusing a competitor's claim is not permitted by
this package and is not made permissible by its presence in this file. Reusing the *structure* is the
entire point.

## Brands recorded

Grüns, Javy Coffee, Obvi Health, RYZE Superfoods, Spacegoods, The SleepDigest, and a small number of
entries whose brand Foreplay did not resolve.

## Removal

If you represent a brand here and would prefer your creative not be included, open an issue or
contact the repository owner and the entries will be removed. No justification needed.
