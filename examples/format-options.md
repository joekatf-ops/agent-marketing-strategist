# Worked example: format recommendations

Illustrative product: a desk organiser with six separate cable loops. Known use: keeping six
cables in individual positions. Concept: shared-desk workers who repeatedly untangle a cable bundle.
Request: three solution-aware image formats, with options. No comparative timing or customer data.

## Us vs Them

Parallel scenes compare two ways of storing the same six cables.

1. Show a loose bundle beside the six separate loops, with the same cables and camera angle.
2. Compare retrieving one labelled cable from each arrangement using two accurate close-ups.
3. Compare desk reset layouts: loose cables on one surface, the organiser on the other. Show the
   actual footprint without an invented space-saving percentage.

## Benefits Callout

A product hero uses a few short callouts to explain practical reasons to consider it.

1. Top-down view with callouts identifying the six individual positions and visible cable ends.
2. Desk-edge scene with a hand returning a cable to its own loop. Explain that action through labels.
3. Close-up of the actual loops and the same set of six cables, showing the arrangement rather than
   claiming unsupported grip strength or durability.

## Mechanism / how it works

A short visual sequence makes product use understandable.

1. Three panels show a loose bundle, separation into the actual loops, and the completed arrangement.
2. A hand selects one cable while the other five stay in their shown positions. Use a real reference
   for the intended cable and loop dimensions.
3. A desk reset sequence shows each cable returning to its place after use, using matched framing.

Recommendation: Us vs Them, option 1. The supplied product fact is immediately visible and the
comparison can be fair without inventing a speed or reliability claim. These are execution options,
not approved finished copy, rendered images or predicted winners.
