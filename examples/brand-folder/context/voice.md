# Voice

FIXTURE DATA. Acme Trailworks does not exist. See README.md in this folder.

## Principles

- Understated. The product is a sock, and overselling it is the fastest way to sound like everyone
  else in the category.
- Physical rather than abstract. Describe what the foot feels at 4pm, not "all-day comfort".
- Never enthusiastic on the brand's own behalf. State the fact and let the reader react.

## Vocabulary

- Say: damp, ridge, seam, arch, merino, wool, walk, all day, by the afternoon.
- Avoid: performance, engineered, technology, optimised, adventure, journey, gear, crush it.
- "Hiking" is fine. "Trail" as an adjective is not, despite the brand name.

## Cadence and reading level

Short sentences. Plain words. Grade 6 to 8. One idea per sentence, and full stops rather than
commas holding two ideas together.

## Approved examples

- "By 4pm your socks have gone damp and started to move. You have probably stopped noticing."
- "A flat toe seam, so there is no ridge sitting across your toes."
- "412 reviews, 4.6 average."

## Rejected examples

- "Elevate your hiking experience with performance merino." Two banned words, and it sounds like
  every other brand.
- "Say goodbye to blister hell." Blister claim, refused as claim-004, and the register is wrong.
- "Australian made merino excellence." Factually wrong on manufacture.

## Banned language and punctuation

- No em dashes or en dashes. Comma, colon, or two sentences.
- No exclamation marks.
- Never the word "just" as a softener.

## Speaker variants

- **Brand voice:** flat, factual, second person.
- **Founder voice:** available and first person, used for why the sock was made rather than for
  claims about what it does.
- **Creator voice:** allowed to be warmer than the brand and must stay inside the claim ceiling. A
  creator saying "no blisters" is still a blister claim.
