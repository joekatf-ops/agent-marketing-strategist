# Visual system

FIXTURE DATA. Acme Trailworks does not exist. See README.md in this folder.

## Asset locations

- `assets/product/`: real product photography on plain backgrounds. The reference for any
  generated image.
- `assets/lifestyle/`: feet, boots, walking, in real places.
- `assets/logo/`: wordmark only, no icon.

## Colours and typography

- Charcoal `#2B2B2B` for text. Warm off-white `#F2EDE4` for backgrounds.
- One accent, rust `#A4551F`, used for a single element per asset and never for body text.
- One sans-serif family throughout. Weight carries hierarchy, not a second family.

## Photography and composition

- Daylight, overcast or open shade. No studio flash and no golden-hour styling.
- Feet in context: boots half-laced, a doorway, a step, the sock actually on a foot.
- Wool texture must be legible. If the knit is not visible the shot is not doing its job.
- Backgrounds are plain or genuinely ordinary. No mountain vistas.

## Distinctive brand assets

- The rust arch band, which is the one recognisable element on the product.
- Close crops at the toe seam.

## Prohibited treatments

- **No before-and-after of a foot, in any form.** It reads as a medical outcome and the brand has
  no evidence for one.
- No visible skin damage, blisters, redness or bandaging. That is the refused claim carried in an
  image, and the visual claim gate applies to pixels exactly as it applies to words.
- No summit shots, no athletic framing, no motion blur suggesting running.
- No fabricated product. A generated image may light, place and crop a real product photograph. It
  may not invent the product's construction, colourway or label.
- No text baked into a generated image. Text is added in the ad tool where it can be corrected.

## Placement and accessibility rules

- Build 4:5 first, and keep the top and bottom 250 pixels clear of anything load-bearing so a
  Reels crop does not take the point.
- Text contrast at least 4.5 to 1 against whatever sits behind it.
- Legible at thumbnail size. If the toe seam is the idea, the seam has to read at 120 pixels wide.
