# Brand core

FIXTURE DATA. Acme Trailworks does not exist. See README.md in this folder.

## Verified facts

- One product line, sold direct in Australia only.
- The Ridgeline Merino Crew is 39 AUD a pair, 99 AUD for three.
- 68 percent Australian merino, knitted offshore. The wool origin is Australian; the manufacture
  is not.
- The toe seam is flat. This is a construction fact anybody can check by holding the sock.
- 412 reviews on the Crew, 4.6 average.

## Founder hypotheses

Not customer-validated. Do not write these as facts.

- That the people who care most are not first-time hikers but people who walk a lot in ordinary
  life and have quietly given up on socks lasting.
- That the flat toe seam is the thing people notice first and the merino is what makes them
  repurchase.

## Positioning

- **Category frame:** a sock you stop thinking about, not technical outdoor equipment.
- **Who:** adults in Australia who walk far enough that their feet become the limiting factor. Day
  hikers, but also nurses, tradespeople and anyone on their feet all day.
- **Primary problem:** by the afternoon their socks have gone damp and started to move, and they
  have accepted that as normal.
- **Promise:** you get to the end of the day without your feet being the thing you noticed.
- **Mechanism:** merino handles moisture instead of holding it, and the flat toe seam removes the
  ridge that a standard sock puts across the toes.
- **Differentiator:** one arch band that fits, rather than graded compression sizing that mostly
  fits nobody.

## Boundaries

- Never a medical or podiatric claim. Not blisters, not circulation, not foot health. The category
  reaches for blister prevention constantly and this brand does not have the evidence.
- Never "Australian made". The wool is Australian and the sock is not.
- Never performance or athletic framing. The reader is walking, not competing.
- No manufactured urgency. Neither offer has an end date, so nothing may be written as expiring.
