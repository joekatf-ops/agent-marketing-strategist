# Frozen Example: Hook Batch

This is a six-option pre-production set for the SLA execution inside `CONTST004`. It remains one
launch ad. Selecting one package does not create six ads.

## 1. Batch header

- Brand: Fieldnote Carry
- Market: Australia
- Product: SnapGrid Cable Pouch
- Coordinate key: `remote-workers__cable-search`
- CONTST test ID: `CONTST004`
- Source: NNT
- Who: remote workers who carry charging gear between home and shared workspaces
- Primary Problem: finding the required cable means searching through a mixed pouch
- Awareness code and job: SLA, differentiation
- Messaging route: proof that can be seen
- Primary hook: Hook 2, "Same six cables. Two very different ways to find one"
- Media type: VIDEO
- Execution format: Comparison
- Execution variant: controlled side-by-side same-item demonstration
- Controlled FORMAT token: `COMPARISON`
- Destination: `PDP`, `https://fieldnotecarry.example/products/snapgrid-cable-pouch`
- CTA: Shop Now
- Complete final ad name:
  `CONTST004_NNT_REMOTE_WORKERS_CABLE_SEARCH_SLA_COMPARISON_PDP_POSTIDXXX`
- Production constraints: one tabletop set, hands only, phone camera, two pouches, six common cables

## 2. Evidence and claim gate

- `EVD-PROD-001`, verified product fact: transparent mesh divider and six elastic cable loops
- `EVD-MKT-021`, market evidence: 63 category reviews from six competitor pages
- `EVD-MKT-022`, market evidence: 21 reviewed comments mention digging, tangles or forgotten cables
- `EVD-CLAIM-006`, approved wording: "See your cables at a glance."
- Prohibited: waterproof, indestructible, fits every charger, prevents forgetting, customer results

## 5. Recommended production order

1. Hook 2: highest contrast and learning value, after the fairness check.
2. Hook 1: clean product-proof control with the lowest production risk.
3. Hook 6: distinct silent-first opening with a visible proof object.
4. Hook 4: strong Who situation with modest production needs.
5. Hook 5: valuable category reframe after claim-owner review.
6. Hook 3: useful language route, but less direct proof than the demonstrations.

Hook 2 is selected. The approved production record uses its primary hook and launches only
`CONTST004_NNT_REMOTE_WORKERS_CABLE_SEARCH_SLA_COMPARISON_PDP_POSTIDXXX` for this SLA execution.
