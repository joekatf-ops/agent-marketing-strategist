## 2. The job

Move the belief that a pouch with more pockets is the same as a pouch where the cable is visible.

## 3. The three-part opening

- Visual hook: 0.0s split screen, two pouches closed, identical framing. 0.5s both hands unzip
- Spoken hook: "Same six cables. Two very different ways to find one."
- On-screen anchor: "Searching vs seeing"

All three express one idea: identical contents, different retrieval.

### Opening gate

- Opening type: open loop. The line names both sides and withholds which is which, so the frame has
  to be watched to resolve it
- Must-have carriers:
  - Emotion: recognition, carried by the left hand still searching at 1.2s
  - Curiosity gap: carried by the spoken line withholding the outcome
  - High stakes: carried by the anchor naming the cost as searching
- Non-negotiables: no prior context, action at 0.0s with both hands already moving, two legible
  panels and nothing else on screen

Frame one is mid-action. Nothing is spent establishing what a cable pouch is.

## 5. Shot list and production needs

People, assets and location: one hand model, SnapGrid Cable Pouch, one plain comparison pouch, two
matched sets of six common cables, locked tabletop camera, neutral desk, single soft key light.

Capture in this order, not story order:

1. Locked-camera split-screen plates, both pouches closed, identical framing. Shoot both sides in one
   take so the light matches
2. Both unzips, three takes
3. Left-hand search, one long take of at least 20 seconds so the edit can choose the duration
4. Right-hand immediate selection, three takes
5. Overhead divider and loop detail, slow finger trace
6. Full repeat of the split screen for the proof beat
7. Closed-to-open selection sequence
8. Product front on for the close

## 8. Rationale

Comparison because the messaging route is proof that can be seen, and a comparison is the only format
where the SLA differentiation job is settled in front of the viewer rather than asserted.

The proof beat repeats the comparison under stated identical conditions, which is what stops it being
a rigged demonstration. Same cables, same start, same camera.

The objection beat pre-empts the obvious reply, that a pouch with more pockets would solve this. It
is answered by reframing the category from storage to visibility, which is the belief in section 2.

The measured rates in `references/08-formats.md` put us versus them comparison at 6.52 percent
against a 5 percent baseline, modestly above. It is chosen here on route fit rather than rate: the
route requires visible proof and no higher-rate format delivers it.
