# Product-only square image ad

Fictional test product, not a customer finding or a measured winner.

## Request

Make one image ad for a desk cable organiser that holds six cables. No research, photo or brand kit.

## Delivery example in a text-only host

I have not generated an image in this text-only session. Here is the finished copy and square brief.

- Main image copy: **Six cables. One place.**
- Supporting image copy: **A desk organiser with room for six.**
- Meta primary text: Give your cables a place on the desk. This organiser holds six.
- Meta headline: A place for six cables
- CTA: Shop now

Square text-led composition. Large primary line above a simple illustration of six cable paths
converging into six orderly lines. Do not depict an invented product housing. Warm white background,
charcoal type and a single cobalt accent are provisional design choices, not an existing brand kit.

## Ready-to-paste prompt

Create a 1:1 square Meta image ad. Text-led editorial design, warm white background, charcoal type,
one cobalt accent. The upper half has the exact primary line "Six cables. One place." In smaller
type beneath it, "A desk organiser with room for six." The lower half shows six simple abstract
cable paths becoming orderly. This graphic illustrates organisation; do not depict a product
housing or claim a particular attachment system. One clear reading order, generous edge spacing,
readable at mobile size. No logo, price, stars, reviews, badges, offer or extra copy.

## Why this works as a starting point

The capacity is supplied. The design communicates that detail without inventing adhesive, material,
compatibility or customer beliefs. Customer research could refine the next message but is not needed
to produce this complete draft. An image-capable host should generate and inspect the asset, not
stop at this text-only handoff.
