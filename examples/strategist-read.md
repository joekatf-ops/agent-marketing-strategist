# Frozen Example: Strategist Read

The request was five more headlines for the PDA carousel in `CONTST004`. The headline is not the
constraint, so the read says that first and then answers the underlying need.

Same brand and coordinate as the other frozen examples.

## 1. The read

The headlines are fine and the offer is the problem. This is a 49 AUD accessory being sold to a
solution-aware reader with no reason to buy today rather than in three weeks, and no answer to the
one objection that actually stops the purchase: they already own a pouch that technically works.
Five more headlines will change nothing, because every version of them is answering a question the
reader has already accepted. What is missing is a reason the switch is worth 49 dollars now.

## 2. What is working

The comparison route is right and it is proven inside this batch. The SLA execution settles the
visibility argument in frame rather than asserting it, and `EVD-PROD-001` supports every load-bearing
feature, so nothing in the creative is doing compliance damage.

"Pockets are not visibility" is the strongest line in the batch. It separates two things the reader
had merged, which is the entire diagnosis, in four words. Keep it.

## 3. What is costing you

1. **No reason to act now, and the ad is at the decision stage.** PDA is where the offer carries the
   argument, and there is no offer in this execution. The reader agrees with you and closes the tab.
   The carousel currently ends on a product feature, which is a solution-aware ending on a
   product-aware ad.
2. **The switching objection is never named.** The reader owns a pouch. It works, badly. Nothing in
   the batch acknowledges that replacing a functioning item requires more justification than buying a
   first one, so the argument lands as "this is better" rather than "this is worth replacing yours."
   `EVD-MKT-022` has 21 comments describing the failure state, which is the raw material for that
   argument and is currently unused at PDA.
3. **The price is unframed.** 49 AUD appears with nothing beside it. At this awareness state a price
   without a frame is compared against zero, which is the reader keeping what they have.
4. **Four headlines all select the same buyer.** They differ in mechanism, which is correct craft, but
   all four assume the reader has already decided to buy something. None reaches the reader who is
   deciding whether to bother.

## 4. What I would do

Fix the offer, not the headline. In order:

- Give the PDA execution a real reason to act now. A verified bundle, a stated guarantee window or a
  first-order term, whichever the brand can actually approve. This is the highest-value change
  available and it is not a copy change, so it needs the claim owner rather than a writer.
  `[OFFER: needs approved terms and an owner]`
- Add one objection-handling frame to the carousel, in the reader's own language, drawn from
  `EVD-MKT-022`. Something in the shape of "I already have a pouch" answered with the visibility
  mechanism rather than with a benefit claim.
- Frame the price against the thing being replaced rather than against nothing.
  `[PRICE FRAME: confirm the comparison is fair and supportable]`
- Then, if you still want more headlines, write two rather than five, and aim them at the
  not-yet-decided reader. Four is already enough for the decided one.

If only one of these happens, make it the first. The offer is doing none of the work an offer is
supposed to do at this awareness state.

## 5. What I am assuming

- That 49 AUD is the current price and there is no active promotion. Not supplied in the brief, and
  if a promotion exists the first recommendation may already be solved. `[PRICE: confirm]`
- That the brand can approve a guarantee or bundle term at all. If it cannot, the ranking changes:
  objection handling becomes the top item and the price frame second.
- That the PDA carousel currently has no offer frame. Read from the batch manifest rather than from
  the finished asset, so if the asset already carries one, disregard item 1.
- That `EVD-MKT-022` is usable at PDA. It is market evidence, not this brand's own customers, so it
  can supply language and cannot supply a prevalence claim.
- Ranking is strategist judgement. `[UNSOURCED, strategist judgement]` The evidence supports that the
  objection exists; it does not establish that the offer outranks the objection, which is my read
  rather than a measured result.
